The PCB

Initialising trace routing...
SUBSTRATE
FR4

Glass-reinforced epoxy laminate. 1.6mm standard. 0.8mm for flexible boards. Fire-rated UL94-V0.

COPPER WEIGHT
1 oz/ft²

35µm copper traces. Thicker copper (2oz) for high-current LED power rails and USB 3.x traces.

MCU
RP2040

Dual-core Cortex-M0+ at 133 MHz. 264KB SRAM. Runs QMK, KMK, or custom firmware. USB 1.1 FS.

LAYERS
2–4

Most keyboards: 2-layer. Premium boards: 4-layer for cleaner RF and dedicated ground plane.

// feel.wav

Sound & feel

Click a switch type to hear and feel the difference. Waveforms are illustrative models of the acoustic profile.

LINEAR

Smooth from top to bottom. No tactile bump. Preferred for gaming. Sound: low thock.

TACTILE

Tactile bump before actuation. No audible click. Typing-focused. Sound: mid thock with pre-travel bump.

CLICKY

Tactile bump + audible click mechanism. Cherry MX Blue, Kailh Box White. Loud. Office-unfriendly.

HALL EFFECT

Magnetic sensor. No physical contact at actuation. Adjustable actuation point 0.1–4.0 mm. No wear.
